# Decisions (MAKU, the interlude mode)

One line each, newest last. Reason after the dash.

2026-10-09 docs/decisions.md did not exist (the brief said it had 3 lines); started it here - nothing to preserve.
2026-10-09 MAKU lives in firmware/src/maku.c, included by seq.c, and plays through the sequencer's step path (maku_step replaces the stored step) - no new pattern data, no project format change.
2026-10-09 MAKU state is not saved in projects or settings (runtime only) - G_COUNT, P_COUNT and the MENU prefs are fixed by the formats and the editor protocol; a mode that starts off is also the safe default.
2026-10-09 Toggle is MENU > SYSTEM > MAKU (inserted before CALIBRATION, which are the two value-less rows) - one existing, tested path; ON initialises the four tracks like a preset load, so it replaces the sounds and patterns (undo copy of a sound load applies).
2026-10-09 DENSITY is HOME's KNOB 1 while MAKU is on, and MIDI CC20 on any channel - one level deep, no new page, no protocol change; the header shows DENSITY n.
2026-10-09 Roles by track: 1 KICK (DRUM, 16 steps), 2 DRONE (ANALOG SOFT PAD, 57 steps), 3 DRONE (ANALOG PWM STR, 13 steps), 4 ARP (ANALOG SINE KEY, 16 steps) - 57 = 3 x 19 and 13 are coprime, so the drones meet in only 6 phases over 741 steps.
2026-10-09 Kick rungs at DENSITY 4 / 40 / 80 (step 0, +step 8, +steps 4 and 12), each new rung fades in by chance instead of switching - the pulse should grow, not step; velocity 30..110 with a wander that grows with density.
2026-10-09 Arp = a 32-place table (hash of the place -> scale degree), a random place, a run of 3..8 steps, then a jump; every note goes through maku_note() which only returns scale degrees - in-key by construction, no snapping needed.
2026-10-09 "Loosen" = per note re-roll chance DENSITY/3 %, runs shorter, arp swing up to 24 %, velocity wander; "tidy" = the same hash table with none of it, so lowering DENSITY brings the same phrases back.
2026-10-09 ROOT / SCALE: an edit on any track becomes all four's (maku_block), the kick's DRUM TUNE (E1) follows the root folded to -6..+5 semitones - reuses the existing scale mechanism, no new key parameter.
2026-10-09 Ducking: the kick sets a dip (to x0.56 at DENSITY 127, none below 40) on the drones, half of it on the arp, recovering in ~120 ms; applied as one integer multiply on the track level in mix_part, x1.0 exact with MAKU off - golden renders stay bit-identical.
2026-10-09 CPU: cpu/mix/maku_127 added to tests/cpu_baseline.txt (1190 instr/sample, the heaviest existing mix is 1589) - ducking is one integer multiply per part per block (not measured separately).
2026-10-09 Step counts: kick 16 (the time axis), drone 57, shimmer 13, arp 29 - all different and none a multiple of 16, so nothing realigns; the arp's last 5 steps of 29 are silent (a breath once per 29 steps against the kick's 16).
2026-10-09 Written material instead of hashes: six open drone voicings (root+5th+octave, sus2, sus4, wide minor 10th, a 2nd-cluster), a six-note shimmer cycle with 5th dyads, a 32-place arp phrase with breaths (255 = rest) - ambient is wide intervals, no thirds in the first rows, space; the loosen step re-rolls notes, the tidy step plays the phrase as written.
2026-10-09 Sounds: PHYS DRONE STRING (drone), GRAIN CLOUD PAD (shimmer), PHYS KALIMBA (arp), a long round DRUM kick - physical-model and granular voices instead of the ANALOG synth presets, which read as a plain synth.
2026-10-09 REL 70 / 70 / 50 (drone, shimmer, arp): measured with the real mixer, REL 85+ at DENSITY 127 fills all 8 voices (151 steals in 12 bars), 70 peaks at 7 with no steal - tests/maku_test.c VOICES keeps it.
2026-10-09 CPU: cpu/mix/maku_127 rebaselined to 1739 (PHYS + GRAIN cost more than the ANALOG presets); about 30 % of the device at the repo's 1.7 % per 100 instructions, 10 % over the heaviest earlier mix (1584) - accepted, headroom is still ~70 %.
2026-10-10 INSERT (Felucca 1.5, fx.c track_insert) is taken from upstream on a trial: the DSP, five track parameters before P_E0 (so stores map by count) and the PHASER's helpers from upstream's perform.c; no pages (MAKU has none) - the drones get one per world (PHASR / CHOR, FLANG / CHOR, or none), the two TONE-macro slots that were free carry its MIX.
2026-10-10 INSERT measured on the target build (JieLi clang, pi32v2): image +2036 B (411512 of 573372), RAM +112 B (80980 of 98304), pool +2320 B (304820 of 344064); the audio ISR's loop cost unchanged (+15 instructions), ins_drive 277 / ins_swept 230 as upstream. Host: +14..20 % of cpu/mix/maku_127 with PHASR on both drones at MIX 100 % (noisy box) - about +5 points of the device's 30 %, not measured on the device.
2026-10-10 INSERT is kept only if it earns its place by ear and by load; if not, delete it: `git revert` the commit "MAKU: the drones get an INSERT per world" (wiring, macro slots, test), then the one before it ("INSERT (Felucca 1.5)": fx.c block + helpers, core.h P_ITYPE..P_IMIX and ins_run, params.c rows, tests/insert_test.c, the test constants P_E0 96 / P_COUNT 104 / 28 spare); that frees 2036 B of flash, 2.4 KB of RAM and the per-block cost.
2026-10-10 tools/size_fns.py skips files MAKU removed (editor*.c) - the target build had not run since the editor was dropped; with it ./build.sh's compile and link steps pass (hardware still untested).
2026-10-10 tests/target_budget.txt rewritten from the MAKU + INSERT target build (ISR 37502 -> 42691, rev_room 218 -> 401 are MAKU's reverb and buses; the rest within a few %); slice_render / slc_rev are OPTIONAL (SLICE is off in this build) - the static check had drifted since MAKU and could not run at all until the build linked.
2026-10-10 Held voices (docs/VOICES_RATING.md): maku.art[i] (STRK SOFT SWEL BOW HOLD) and maku_is_tie() make the floor and the haze hold a note across steps with ST_TIE; seq_step asks maku_is_tie for the next step (the stored steps are empty in MAKU) - a pure function of (track, step), so the sequencer and maku_step agree.
2026-10-10 The held floor and haze run at DIV 1/8 (a chord about every 7 s) - a swell of 1.4..2 s has to arrive before the chord changes; struck voices keep 1/16.
2026-10-10 Voice shares (maku_voice_cap, read by trk_nvoice): floor 3 (PHYS held: 2), haze 2 when held, arp 2, kick as before - the held parts sound all the time, and without a cap 8 voices ran out (cross-part kills) in 16 of 120 worlds; with it, 0. Counted with voice_kills, not with "a note-on while all 8 were busy", which also counts a part replacing its own voice.
2026-10-10 Voice catalog (maku_voices.c) replaces the preset lists in maku_world: 9 floors, 5 hazes, 10 voices, each an engine + preset + articulation + overrides + its own TONE macro (ANALOG's E6 is DRV, PHYS's is BOW: one shared TONE row would distort). Raise MAKU_CATALOG_VERSION when a row or weight changes - the emulator's rating log keys on it.
2026-10-10 Emulator rating bar: -2..+2 stores the whole state (seed, catalog, voices, 12 macros, every raw parameter, held buttons, world age) via web_snapshot; localStorage is a convenience, JSON / CSV export is the record; #seed=N replays a world. No keyboard shortcut (the number row and letters are the buttons and keys).
2026-10-10 Kick: the four on the floor is certain from DENSITY 96 (rungs 4 / 40 / 68, was 80 and only full at 127); above it DENSITY adds noise ornaments on the kick track's own DRUM lanes (open hat on the off-beats from 96, closed hat on the other 16ths from 104) instead of more kick; the black keys are eight noise pads (MAKU_PAD) instead of pattern operations (maku_kick_op removed). Above 96 the voice shares change (maku_voice_cap): kick 2 (it and a hat), floor 2, arp 1 - with the held parts' shares there was no room otherwise (steals in 73 of 120 worlds); the VOICES test no longer asks for one voice spare, only for no voice taken from another part and all 8 busy in under 1 % of the blocks. A clap ornament was tried and left out: the voices did not fit.
2026-10-10 Verbs hold three parameters of their own while the button is down (maku.vp[VB_*][3], shown in the three quadrants, moved by SELECT / PRESETS / ALGORITHM; ui_input.c maku_verb_now / maku_verb_turn, ui_maku.c mq_verb) - HOME has none, REC is a toggle and has none; implemented for PLAY, GLO and SCL first, the other buttons wait for the new verbs (docs/AMBIENT.md, proposal).
2026-10-10 PLAY is SINK / WASH / HUSH: the reverb combs (ROOM size to 0.99, SPRING gain to 0.985) feed back harder as the dive deepens (fx.c, maku_sink) - the existing dive ramp (~1.5 s) carries it, so nothing new runs while PLAY is up; defaults 100 / 127 / 127 keep the old BREAK bit for bit.
2026-10-10 SCL tapped steps MAJ MIN LYD DOR MIX PHRY PEN MPEN (major, minor in turn); a scale off the list restarts at MAJ - held, it is still the key picker, and its three knobs are ROOT / SCALE / REG (the drones' register, an octave either way).
2026-10-10 GLO lost the tracks' gain (KNOB 1..4) and the SOLO keys (ui_layer.c, perform.c is left, perf_solo stays 0) - the mutes, UNMUTE ALL and TAP TEMPO stay; its three knobs are BPM / SWING / DUCK (DUCK 64 = the old dip). ui_test's GLO / #102 / #119 / #126 checks follow.
2026-10-10 ui_draw.c: in MAKU the GLO and SCL layer maps are no longer drawn (the quadrants show the verb's parameters), the layers' logic is unchanged.
2026-10-10 KEEP / SCRAMBLE / CASCADE / FOG / TWIST on SAVE / SEQ / ARP / FX / EDIT; ENV and LFO get none; the old verbs (FREEZE, TAPE, SWEEP, REPEAT, harmonizer, riser, ARP toggle) are gone from maku_buttons (perform.c is untouched, FOG uses its LPF macro perf_k[0]).
2026-10-10 Held-verb amounts (maku.va, Q12) ramp in maku_block (up 16 / down 8 a block); the sends are added in fx.c mix_part through maku_boost (x1.0 exact with MAKU off, golden renders unchanged); TWIST moves the macros' effective value (maku_macro_set adds it, maku.m keeps the knob's own); KEEP records the arp's last 16 steps (kr / kv) and the drones' last steps (dc) in maku_step.
2026-10-10 CASCADE's first parameter is DENSE (chance a step plays), not RATE as proposed - the step division would move the 29-step phrase against the others.
2026-10-10 SWELL (ENV) and WOBBLE (LFO) added: they move track parameters (P_ATK, P_REL, P_LRATE, P_LD_FLT of tracks 2..4) by an offset on top of a remembered base (maku_over): a value written by a macro or a world meanwhile becomes the new base, so letting go puts back what is current, not what was there at the press. SWELL's REV and WOBBLE's CHORUS are sends / levels added at mix time (maku_swell_rvs, maku_boost).

2026-10-10 DRIFT replaces FOG on FX: while held, the 12 macros (REACH 43+: also the space, 86+: tempo, SWING, DUCK) take a random-walk step now and then, within RANGE/2 of the press (tempo ±16 BPM max, not on MIDI clock), and stay where they land until the next world; the space it adds (maku.drs) raises the non-kick sends and closes the master low-pass and also stays. FOG's WASH / SPREAD / DARK are gone.

2026-10-11 Verbs regrouped (docs/VERBS_PLAN.md): the upper row FX SCL ENV LFO EDIT GLO holds six verbs of change, one axis each: SINK (the old BREAK), CASCADE (+ HARM, a third above; ECHO dropped), SWELL (+ STAY; REV dropped), WOBBLE (DEPTH also drives the chorus; + PITCH), LIFT (TONE / EDGE / THIN, replaces TWIST), SCRAMBLE. KEEP is gone. The lower row keeps: ARP is the key (the layer SCL moved there), SEQ the tempo (the layer GLO), PLAY a tap switch for DRIFT with its green LED (hold: its parameters, a knob turned = no toggle), SAVE and REC do nothing. DRIFT takes a value turned by hand as its new anchor (it is on for long now, and would pull it back); a new world switches it off. The master's filter (perf_k[0]) is THIN's high-pass minus DRIFT's darkening.

2026-10-11 SINK goes deeper: the reverb combs now feed back towards 0.995 (ROOM 32620) and the spring towards 0.99 (32500), and the dive also lengthens the drones' and arp's ATK by up to +40 (maku_block, through maku_over, WASH's share), so the notes lose their bite as the world sinks; both lift with the dive.

2026-10-11 SINK stronger still: the dive ramps in ~0.75 s (was ~1.5 s), the reverb send rises to full and the delay send to 0.8, the ATK lift is up to +80.

2026-10-11 SINK is a wash: while the dive is on the dry voices (not the kick) step back to ~40 % (pan gains x dg in mix_part) and the reverb send is levelled (rev_level, fx.c: gain = long-term level / quick level, x0.25..x4, as far as SINK says), so the loudness evens out in the wash. Idle (no dive) it is not touched.
2026-10-11 The MAKU pulse's kick is DRUM KICK 2 (PULSE, new): TONE crossfades a thick, long, round kick (below 80) into an ECG monitor's beeps, a sine beep (~600 Hz, one 50 ms pip a hit; TUNE moves it; DRV only drives the kick's share; the kick's body shortens from TONE 8 to 40 for a tight cut), from TONE 80 it fades in, pure from 110 up; from TONE 100 the beep climbs (up to +4 semitones, an octave above it fading in: a monitor's alarm tone); the drive stays on the kick. KICK PUNCH/ROUND are unchanged; PULSE sits outside DVT_COUNT so the per-voice tests keep their tables (tests/drum_test.c pulse()).
